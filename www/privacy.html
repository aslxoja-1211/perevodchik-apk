HopTell — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 21, 2026 | Application: HopTell (com.ovozli.tarjimon)

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how HopTell ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your personal data, voice audio, and permissions. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

HopTell is an instantaneous bilingual face-to-face voice translator. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data or voice recordings.

2. Permissions and Audio Data (RECORD_AUDIO)

To provide live real-time voice translation, HopTell requires access to your device's microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO).

Core Guarantee: Microphone access is used strictly and solely while you press and hold or actively trigger the microphone button. We do NOT record background audio in the background or when the app is closed.

  • Transient Processing: Your speech audio is captured locally and transmitted via encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.3) solely for transcription and translation.
  • No Permanent Audio Storage: Audio clips are processed in memory and are immediately discarded once the translation is complete. We do NOT store audio files on remote servers.

3. Information We Collect and Process

  • Voice and Speech Input: Processed ephemerally to generate text translations.
  • Translated Text: Displayed on screen and stored only locally on your device's private storage (temporary offline cache) for zero-latency instant replay. It is never uploaded to permanent external databases.
  • Device & Network Information: Basic anonymous network status (online/offline) to inform you of internet availability.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

To deliver world-class translation and neural voice synthesis, HopTell uses secure cloud infrastructure:

  • Google Gemini API: Used for multilingual speech recognition, text translation, and studio neural speech synthesis. Google processes data pursuant to Google Cloud's data privacy commitments.
  • Vercel Cloud Network: Used as a secure serverless gateway to protect API keys and manage rate limits.

Neither we nor our service providers use your private conversations to train public generative AI models.

5. Data Security

All data exchanged between HopTell and cloud translation servers is encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS (Transport Layer Security) encryption. We enforce zero API key exposure on client devices.

6. Children's Privacy

HopTell does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

7. Managing Your Permissions

You can revoke microphone permission at any time through your Android device settings:

Settings → Apps → HopTell → Permissions → Microphone → Deny

Note: If microphone permission is denied, voice translation will be unavailable, but text editing and viewing will continue to function.
